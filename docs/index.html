§ Overview

Zortzi-TTS is a Basque text-to-speech system fine-tuned from the Audio8-TTS-0.6B preview model. It was trained using a two-phase curriculum on Mozilla Common Voice 26.0 (Basque, 134K clips) and the HiTZ Aholab TTS corpus, producing two natural-sounding voices: Maider (female) and Antton (male).

The model captures Basque-specific phonotactics and prosody, including correct rising intonation on yes/no questions, focal pitch peaks on wh-question words, and expressive exclamatory contours. On this page we compare four deployment configurations: the original PyTorch model (GPU), ONNX FP16 and INT4 exports (CPU), and Piper TTS (a lightweight VITS baseline trained on the same voices).

§ Key Features

🗣️

Two Natural Voices

Maider (female) and Antton (male), anchored from HiTZ Aholab professional recordings.

🎵

Correct Basque Prosody

Rising intonation on yes/no questions, focus peaks on wh-words, and expressive exclamations.

⚡

Multiple Deploy Targets

PyTorch (GPU), ONNX FP16, and ONNX INT4 (578 MB) for CPU deployment — no GPU required.

📚

Large Training Corpus

134K Common Voice + 41K HiTZ clips (with 3× upsampled interrogatives/exclamatives).

🎛️

44.1 kHz Output

Full-band audio at CD-quality sample rate, far surpassing typical 22 kHz neural TTS.

🔧

Reproducible Pipeline

Complete manifest builder, training scripts, and ONNX export toolchain included.

§ Training Methodology

Phase 1 — Phonotactics

Common Voice 26.0 Basque (134K clips). Trains the model on Basque sound patterns without reference audio. Stopped at step 6,000 to preserve prosodic flexibility.

6,000 steps · 64 min

Phase 2 — Voice + Prosody

HiTZ Aholab corpus (41K clips) with 3× upsampled interrogatives and exclamatives. Anchors Maider & Antton voices while polishing question intonation.

7,746 steps · 98 min

ONNX Export & Quantization

Custom ONNX export (opset 17) with manual attention and KV-cache. Two-pass INT4 quantization: MatMulNBits (weights) + GatherBlockQuantized (embeddings).

578 MB total · INT4

Piper Baseline

Separately trained Piper VITS medium models for the same voices. Lightweight (15.7M params) and extremely fast on CPU, but with lower audio quality (22 kHz).

15.7M params · 61 MB
